# Licensing and Asset-Source Evidence

Checked: **2026-09-20**

This page records external source material used by [Modder Resources](../reference/modder-resources/README.md).

It is a source ledger, not legal advice and not a substitute for the exact licence/terms attached to an individual asset.

## General licence references

### Creative Commons licence conditions

Official licence overview:
https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/cclicenses/

Supports the meanings of BY, SA, NC and ND and the six standard Creative Commons copyright licences.

### CC0 1.0

Official deed:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Supports that CC0 dedicates/waives copyright and related rights to the extent allowed and permits copying, modification and distribution, including commercial use, without permission.

### SPDX License List

https://spdx.org/licenses/

Canonical identifiers and licence-text references for software/content licences. Useful for dependency/provenance records.

### Mozilla Public License 2.0 FAQ

https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/FAQ/

Supports the description of MPL 2.0 as file-level copyleft.

### GNU GPL

https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl.en.html

Primary GPLv3 licence/source material. Strong-copyleft integration questions should be reviewed against the actual licence and facts rather than summarized from memory.

## Marketplace terms

### Fab Standard License

https://www.fab.com/eula

Checked claim:

- project use and modification are permitted under the Standard License;
- projects containing Fab assets may be distributed commercially;
- standalone resale/redistribution of the asset is prohibited;
- Personal and Professional pricing tiers have the same scope of rights;
- Reference-Only access does not provide source-format content.

Always check the individual listing for its actual licence.

### Unity Asset Store Terms and EULA

https://unity.com/legal/as-terms

Current page checked 2026-09-20; page states last update 2024-12-04.

Checked claim:

- non-Restricted assets may be embedded in a larger Licensed Product subject to the EULA;
- Restricted Assets use their own Restricted Asset Terms;
- separate provider EULAs can apply;
- the standard terms do not create a general right to redistribute Asset Store source packages as standalone assets.

## Open / free asset sources

### Poly Haven

https://polyhaven.com/license

Checked claim: Poly Haven states that its HDRIs, textures and 3D models are CC0 and may be used, modified and redistributed without required attribution.

### Kenney

https://kenney.nl/support

Checked claim: Kenney states game assets on its asset pages are CC0 and attribution is not required.

### OpenGameArt

https://opengameart.org/node/5571

Checked claim: OpenGameArt hosts art under multiple supported licences; the exact asset licence and attribution requirements must be checked.

### Freesound

https://freesound.org/help/faq/

Checked claim: Freesound currently allows uploaders to choose CC0, CC BY or CC BY-NC for sounds; use requirements therefore vary by sound.

### Openverse

https://openverse.org/en-gb/about

Checked claim: Openverse is a discovery/search system aggregating openly licensed/public-domain media. Its own documentation advises independently verifying licence and attribution information before reuse.

### Google Fonts

https://developers.google.com/fonts

Checked claim: Google states the fonts in Google Fonts are released under open-source licences and may be used in commercial and non-commercial projects. Check the exact family licence before redistribution/modification.

## Per-item / custom-licence sources

### Sketchfab

Creative Commons download guidance:
https://sketchfab.com/developers/download-api/guidelines

Marketplace licence:
https://sketchfab.com/licenses

Checked claim: downloadable models can carry Creative Commons licences with attribution obligations; marketplace/editorial models have different terms. Licence must be checked per model.

### Pixabay

https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/

Checked claim: Pixabay permits free use and modification subject to its Content License but prohibits standalone distribution of content.

### itch.io

https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms

Checked claim: publishers retain ownership of their content. itch.io is a distribution platform and does not provide one blanket mod-asset licence; creator/project terms must be checked.

### Quaternius

https://quaternius.com/license.html

Checked 2026-09-20: the general site licence is **Quaternius Asset License (QAL) v1.0**, shown as last updated 2026-08-28.

The current short summary states that assets can be used free of charge in personal, educational and commercial games/projects with no credit required, but the assets themselves may not be resold or redistributed as assets.

Do not rely on older posts describing all Quaternius assets as CC0. Retain/check the terms shipped with the exact asset/version you obtained.

### Adobe Mixamo

FAQ:
https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html

Additional Terms:
https://wwwimages2.adobe.com/content/dam/cc/en/legal/servicetou/Mixamo-Addl-Terms-en_US-20210623.pdf

Adobe-hosted Community licensing FAQ:
https://community.adobe.com/questions-696/mixamo-faq-licensing-royalties-ownership-eula-and-tos-589400

Checked claim: Adobe's FAQ states Mixamo is free with an Adobe ID and characters/animations may be used royalty-free in personal, commercial and non-profit projects including video games. Adobe-hosted Community guidance additionally states that raw character/animation files are not to be redistributed as asset packages or free raw-file distributions. Treat that Community page as explanatory guidance rather than the controlling legal text; Adobe terms remain controlling.
