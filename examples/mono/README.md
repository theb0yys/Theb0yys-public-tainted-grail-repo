# Mono Mod Examples

These examples are for the Mono/BepInEx version of Tainted Grail.

Choose what you want to make:

- [Combat](combat/README.md) — combat pressure, poise, damage/death hooks.
- [Gameplay](gameplay/README.md) — lockpicking, companions, patrols, merchants, survival, crime and more.
- [Items](items/README.md) — granting items and custom weapon presentation.
- [Magic](magic/README.md) — projectile behavior.
- [UI](ui/README.md) — inventory views, HUD changes, map helpers and bonfire menus.
- [Audio](audio/README.md) — footsteps and contextual music.
- [Rendering](rendering/README.md) — fog, weather, sky and combat effects.
- [Infrastructure](infrastructure/README.md) — saves, performance tools and cross-mod communication.
- [Harmony basics](harmony/README.md) — small examples for learning method patches.

If you do not know whether your game is Mono or IL2CPP, go back to [Find the game and identify your setup](../../guides/getting-started/find-game-and-runtime.md).
