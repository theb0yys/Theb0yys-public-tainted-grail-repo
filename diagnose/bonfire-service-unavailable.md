# Bonfire Service Is Unavailable

Do not bypass the native precondition merely because a custom menu can show the option.

Examples of native context checks include:

- open-world/upgraded bonfire for fast travel;
- required bonfire crafting level for handcrafting;
- survival-saving restriction for bonfire save;
- a left-behind pet for recall.

A good custom menu may show the action disabled and explain why it is unavailable.

It should not force the underlying service through a state the native owner considers invalid.
