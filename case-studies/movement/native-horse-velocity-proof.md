# Native Horse Velocity: Proof125

Avalon Mounts isolated one bounded movement capability instead of claiming the whole mount system.

## Proven seam

`VMount.RunningVelocity` and `VMount.TurningVelocity` postfixes.

A live proof exercised **1.25 / 1.25**. A later user smoke exercised mount, movement, transition, save/load and quit/relaunch without Avalon Mounts errors on the observed stack.

## Deliberately excluded

The case did not promote ownership/current-horse pointer, recall, marker, armour, stamina, custom-mount contract or pet-bridge ownership.

## Lesson

A narrow proven scalar seam is more valuable than a broad “mount support” claim that mixes unproven owners.
