# Small Exit Helper Instead of a Dungeon Map

Dungeon Exit Helper answered two questions only:

- where did I come in?
- how do I get back out?

It used scene metadata, hero coordinates and a passive overlay rather than building a minimap or reverse-engineering dungeon geometry.

## Lesson

A narrow QoL request often has a much smaller owner surface than the feature it superficially resembles.

“Point me back to the entrance” does not require owning the map system.
