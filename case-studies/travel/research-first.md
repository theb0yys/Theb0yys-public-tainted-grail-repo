# Research Before Fast-Travel Mutation

Tainted Travel deliberately remained a research scaffold because fast travel crosses map discovery, scene transition, time, quest/story locks, economy, survival, mounts and encounter systems.

Rather than patch a plausible `FastTravel` symbol, the programme first defined:

- exact unknowns;
- adjacent owners;
- read-only outputs;
- dry-run route/cost/time/danger records;
- explicit blocked mutations.

## Lesson

Some domains are too coupled for a useful first implementation to be a live patch. A good public guide should show when **not to implement yet**.
