# Case Authoring Packets

Wave 4 case studies and diagnosis pages must be source-mapped before publication.

Each packet records:

- the reader symptom/question;
- the evidence-backed case history;
- what the evidence proves;
- what it does not prove;
- the canonical system/mechanic pages that own the underlying truth;
- the public case-study page;
- the diagnosis page;
- the public rewrite's own evidence status.
