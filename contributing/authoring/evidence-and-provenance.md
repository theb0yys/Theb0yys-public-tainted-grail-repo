# Evidence and Provenance

Every material public claim should be traceable to an underlying source class even when private implementation details are not published.

Record:

- subject;
- evidence type;
- game/runtime/build scope;
- private source family or maintainer locator;
- proof lane;
- last verified;
- known limits.

Private research reports are maps. Their underlying static/runtime/validation evidence determines the technical claim ceiling.

A public clean-room rewrite inherits **knowledge**, not automatic validation state for a newly authored example.
